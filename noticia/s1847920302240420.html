Halloween 2026 + Gameplay Update

30 out 2026 · ver o post original na Steam

Texto completo (original, em inglês)

Halloween Cauldrons that grant willing trick-or-treaters delicious candy.

New this year - Halloween Cauldrons: Heavy Melee , break the cauldron to pick up a piece of Candy and stash it in your pocket for later. When you're feeling a bit snacky, you can consume the candy to grant yourself a boost of energy with the temporary Sugar Rush buff. - Who said candy is bad for you?

The Halloween festivities are available in-game now through November 2nd, 2026. Happy Haunting!

Gameplay Update

General:

Mid-Boss: Adjusted the terrain layout where the midboss is, the area is a little bigger now and has more cover

Mid-Boss: Health increased by 20%

Mid-Boss: Health gained per minute increased by 20%

Mid-Boss: Resistance reductions are now only 50% as effective against the Mid-Boss

Mid-Boss: Roar now happens at 25% HP instead of 50%

Mid-Boss: Bounty reduced from 3000 (+50/min) to 1500 (+25/min)

Mid-Boss: The Rejuvenator now drops as 3 crystals placed evenly around the Mid-Boss pit (random positions), instead of 1 crystal in the center

Mid-Boss: Each Rejuvenator crystal breaks in 1 heavy melee and grants the team 1 Rejuvenator (total per Mid-Boss kill unchanged at 3)

Mid-Boss: The full heal for breaking a Rejuvenator now applies to each crystal (previously only on the first hit)

Mid-Boss: Rejuvenator crystals now give a bounty to the player that breaks them (500 +9/min)

Mid-Boss: Rejuvenator crystal drop time increased from 6s to 7.5s

Breakables growth values reduced by 10%

Fixed some boxes taking longer to respawn if another box's or powerup's pickup was left on the ground

Haunts base bounty and growth increased by 10%

Haunts HP and Damage output increased by 10%

Haunted Souls secure rate is now 25% slower

Haunted Souls secure rate is now up to 50% faster than baseline based on how far behind you are in Net Worth compared to the enemy team's average (max rate at a 20% Net Worth gap)

Comeback gold from non-hero sources increased from a max of 26% to 30% (when behind by ~20% Net Worth)

The bonus NPC souls multiplier when behind now also increases tick souls for the 2 poorest players

Healing Snacks heal increased from 10% Max HP to 14%

Steam Vents now increase stamina regeneration by 150%

Move slow to air drag ratio increased from 0.35 to 0.5

Move speed in tunnels reduced from +70% to +50%

Haunts - Past Dues: Now channels their special attack, creating a build-up aura on the ground that applies sleep slowly to Heroes standing within it over time. Killing the channeling creature destroys the aura.

Fixed some bugs with Walker stomp logic

Latest 6 heroes are no longer categorized as "new" anymore. This means they have normal hero unlocking requirements for ranked and can now be banned.
